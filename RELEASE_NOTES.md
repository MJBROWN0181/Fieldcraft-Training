# Fieldcraft Training v0.1.0

Initial working release of the technician training workspace.

- Create, publish, schedule, and assign slide-style training lessons.
- Invite technicians by text link; technicians complete their own profiles and can answer lesson questions.
- Track completed courses, licenses, certificates, and supervisor sign-off.
- Keep company policies private; collect each technician's read acknowledgment and create Policy Talk lessons.
- Ask and answer questions on a shared trade board, with a company access switch.
- Invite managers with read-only access and trainers who can manage training without changing company details.

This version is a single-company pilot. A multi-company signup system and automatic SMS/email invitation delivery are planned for later work.
