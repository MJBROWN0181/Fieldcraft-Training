# Fieldcraft Training v0.2.0

A one-company pilot for JLBJ Septic field training. The live site is https://fieldcraft-training.mjbrown0181.chatgpt.site.

## Changes since v0.1.0

- Added a clear landing page for staff sign-in and technician training access.
- Let supervisors choose 1–20 field questions when drafting a lesson with ChatGPT. Pasting the generated lesson fills the editable Field Questions list.
- Added an assignment tracker under Lessons showing who received each lesson, who finished and submitted answers, and who received supervisor sign-off.
- Let supervisors remove a technician from the company roster while preserving their profile and training history. The old company invitation no longer grants access.
- Added a Delete invitation action for pending technician links.

Technicians access lessons through their invitation link. Supervisors manage lessons and records through the live site. This release is source code and a record of the pilot changes; it is not a downloadable native app.
